## 1. Brand Identity & Stature

Prerna Computer Academy is the premier government-authorized computer institute in Degloor, delivering 100% practical, hands-on MS-CIT certification for government employment (Talathi, Police Bharti, ZP, MPSC, Court Clerk), university admissions, and 21st-century digital skills (AI tools, office productivity, cyber safety).

### Brand Personality & Voice
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Official government accreditation, MSBTE and MKCL certified standards.
- **Inspirational & Empowering:** "प्रेरणा" (Inspiration) — uplifting rural and semi-urban students with practical technical skills.
- **Warm & Approachable:** Clear guidance, Marathi & English bilingual communication, zero intimidating jargon.

## 4. Logo Usage System (UI UX Pro Max Brand Specification)

### 4 Official Logo Variants
1. **Full Horizontal Lockup (`logo-full-horizontal`):**
   - For website navigation headers, letterheads, and wide promotional banners.
   - Combines the Golden Aubergine Medallion + `PRERNA COMPUTER ACADEMY` wordmark + `DEGLOOR` badge + `MKCL ALC` accreditation tag.
2. **Emblem Seal (`logo-icon`):**
   - For favicons, mobile header badge, app icons, and touch targets (24px to 128px).
   - Features the outer guilloché ring, Tri-Star summit, `PCA` Monogram, `MKCL ALC` golden plaque, and academic laurel wreath.
3. **Stacked Lockup (`logo-stacked`):**
   - For center-aligned hero sections, official PDF prospectus covers, and square social media avatars.
   - Center-aligned emblem with balanced wordmark and Marathi script below.
4. **Monochrome / Reversed:**
   - Pure white and 24K gold accents for dark Aubergine backgrounds.

### Clear Space Rule
- The minimum clear space around the emblem must equal at least 25% of the mark's diameter ($0.25 \times d$) to preserve institutional dignity and readability.
